# Total Chlorine In-Service

## Project Summary

This de-identified project addresses late time-sensitive operational readings and incomplete exception documentation.

## Problem

Required readings were sometimes taken beyond the established four-hour interval or recorded without complete supporting documentation.

## Solution

Created a standardized in-service and acknowledgement form that:

- Communicates that readings should be taken every four hours
- Encourages completing readings slightly early instead of late
- Explains the documentation required when a justified delay occurs
- Captures staff signatures as evidence of education
